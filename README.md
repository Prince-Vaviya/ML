## 💡 Survival Tips for ML Visitors

1. **Don't skip EDA:** 80% of Machine Learning is cleaning data, 15% is hyperparameter tuning, and 5% is crying over missing imports.
2. **Beware of Data Leakage:** Always split your train and test datasets *before* applying scaling/imputation fit transforms!
3. **Star the repo ⭐** if this helped you pass your ML assignment or saved you from a `KeyError` at 2 AM!
